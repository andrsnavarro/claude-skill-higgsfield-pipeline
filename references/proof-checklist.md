# Proof, not promises, inspection checklist

Do not approve a generation by the model name or by the prompt. Approve it by what is visible, meaning what changed, what held and what is broken. A strong model can still produce a result that is not ready.

## The bar (same every time)
Before anything reaches Seedance, the source must pass.

- **Coherent light.** One believable source, with shadows falling consistently in the declared light direction. In interiors use soft sources, since hard visible rays are usually slop.
- **Tonality with soft falloff.** No crushed shadows or blown highlights. Real tonal transition.
- **Real material.** Surfaces that read as the material they claim to be. No oily or soapy texture (nothing to hold on to, so it melts in motion).
- **Depth and haze.** Atmospheric haze tells the eye a background is far away. Without it everything reads with the same sharpness, which is flat and immediate slop.
- **Complete objects.** Nothing half-formed, twisted or smeared (bent poles, smeared signage, melted storefronts).

## Comparison rule for edits
Everything an edit changes is **compared against the original**.

1. Does the **identity** hold? (silhouette, anatomy, face)
2. Do all the **views agree** (front, back, portrait)?
3. Is the **requested change** consistent everywhere?
4. Did something change that you did **not** ask for? (layout, figure position, a crop that became two, a global tint). That alone is a reason for HOLD.

## The verdict depends on downstream use
The same image can be approved or held depending on what comes next. Example. A sheet with coherent armor but a helmet that hides the face is a **go** if you only need the armor design, and a **hold** if it has to carry the face into Seedance. I always ask or define what the shot is for before giving the verdict.

## Master wide shortcut (b-roll)
When no cut depends on exact off-frame geometry (b-roll, montage, action coverage), a single wide plate can be enough. Mediums and close-ups may invent what the plate never showed, so use the shortcut only when that invention cannot break blocking or continuity.
